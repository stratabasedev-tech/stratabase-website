Purpose-built for the field

Everything starts with
the observation.

Designed around the realities of field logging: intermittent connectivity, deep datasets, ongoing quality checks and the need to keep records traceable.

⌁

Offline-first logging

Capture geological and geotechnical records with locally available projects and synchronise when connectivity returns.

▤

Data Review & Batch Edit

Inspect and correct multiple field records in a structured workspace, with filters and data checks.

◎

QC & traceability

Surface suspect measurements and unresolved exceptions instead of silently changing factual observations.

◈

Multiple investigation types

Work across boreholes, rock-mechanics records, test pits, structural observations and samples.

⇄

Import & export

Move between field records, Excel workbooks and factual outputs with review steps before data changes.

▣

Organised projects

Manage investigation information in a project-focused workspace, with organisational access and ongoing development of project tools.

One connected workflow

From core box
to clear records.

StrataBase is being developed around a continuous record lifecycle. Geological observations should flow from initial capture to review, correction, export and recovery without repeated manual re-entry.
