Data matters

Cloud-connected. Field-first.

Data stays available to a previously authorised device when connectivity drops. Central synchronisation and organisation-level archives provide additional layers of protection when enabled and successfully verified.

Cloud archives cannot include new records that haven't yet synchronised from offline devices.

Private by design

Access belongs to the organisation.

A Google account can establish identity; it doesn't automatically grant project access. StrataBase uses approved organisation membership and access roles.
