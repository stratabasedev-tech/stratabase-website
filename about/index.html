About

Built in the field. For the field.

A practical logging idea that grew into an evolving geotechnical investigation platform.

Why StrataBase exists

Geotechnical investigation data is often collected in the field, reviewed in separate spreadsheets and assembled again for reporting. StrataBase is designed to keep those steps connected without making field capture unnecessarily complicated.

The approach

Structured logging, a practical review interface, offline-capable devices and controlled cloud access. The aim is to make field data more consistent and easier to trace.

Our story

Built in the field. For the field.

Simple for the geologist. Meticulously engineered underneath.

StrataBase began as a small attempt to simplify repetitive Excel logging work. That first tool grew into a dedicated field logger and eventually an evolving platform for geological and geotechnical investigations.

The approach was shaped by working on investigations in the field: capture observations once, keep their context, and make the journey to review and reporting less burdensome.

StrataBase remains an evolving product. We test it in real workflows, keep what works and refine what doesn't. The purpose remains the same: better field records with less unnecessary administration.

A little light from the beginning.

Behind the Core

Every useful idea starts somewhere.

Long before StrataBase had its own website, its earliest ideas were shaped by fieldwork and late evenings spent solving practical problems — sometimes from a tent at a remote site in Namibia.

The Core is our original symbol of that beginning: layers of experience brought together by persistence, curiosity and the drive to build something useful. It is a reminder that good work often begins quietly, far from a polished office.
