The story behind the data

Born out of fieldwork.

StrataBase was shaped by practical geological logging needs: fewer repeated entries, clearer records, usable offline workflows and less time transferring field notes into reporting systems.

Built in the field. For the field.

What began with a focused core-logging workflow is evolving into a broader geological and geotechnical investigation workspace. Field observations lead the design; engineering judgment remains with the professional using the data.

What matters

StrataBase is developed around preserving factual measurements, highlighting questionable records for QC, and ensuring that the information entered in the field remains useful during review and reporting.

Access

The application uses organisation-based access. New accounts require appropriate approval. Only authorised users should enter company or client data.
